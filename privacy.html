Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Piyo Halal is the pre-launch website for a guide to halal-friendly beverages: chai houses, juice bars, coffee shops, and what's actually in the cup. We aim to collect as little data as possible.

What we collect

This site does not set cookies and does not run analytics or advertising scripts. Any form on this site does not transmit data anywhere until we launch; nothing you type leaves your browser.

Hosting

The site is served by our hosting provider, which may log standard technical request data (IP address, user agent) to operate and secure the service.

Changes

If data practices change at launch, this policy will be updated before any collection begins.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details published on this site once we launch.
