Drinks hide more than food does.

Nobody serves a kabab with mystery gelatin, but drinks do it constantly: jelly cubes in falooda, marshmallow foam on hot chocolate, carmine-tinted pink drinks, cream liqueur flavorings in "dessert lattes", wine-based vanilla extracts in syrup. The menu rarely says, and the barista often doesn't know.

Piyo Halal treats every drink like an ingredient list waiting to be read. Each listing in the guide marks a drink either "clear cup" — every questionable ingredient confirmed with the shop — or "ask", with the exact question to ask at the counter.

We're compiling the guide shop by shop, starting in Chicago, and publishing what the shops tell us, in their words.

What we check in the cup

Gelatin
Jelly cubes, marshmallows, some kulfi stabilizers and whipped toppings. Beef, pork, or fish gelatin — the source matters and shops can tell us.
Carmine (E120)
An insect-derived red in some rose syrups, pink drinks, and toppings. Many scholars flag it; every pink drink in the guide states its colorant.
Alcohol-based flavorings
Vanilla and fruit extracts carried in ethanol, plus "rum flavor" syrups. We record what the shop's syrup brand publishes.
Rennet & whey in toppings
Cheese foams and some cream toppings. Microbial or animal rennet — the supplier's spec sheet answers it.

Three kinds of places, one guide.

Chai houses & cafes

Karak, masala chai, Kashmiri pink chai, and the late-night chai spots where the kettle never cools. We note which shops brew with loose leaf and what's in the pink.

Juice & smoothie bars

Fresh-pressed sugarcane, falooda counters, smoothie chains. The fruit is fine — it's the jellies, boba, and whey boosters we read the labels on.

Coffee & dessert drinks

Third-wave shops and dessert lattes. We record which syrup lines the shop pours and what their maker publishes about flavor carriers.
